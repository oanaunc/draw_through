# One unique reference for every lesson — build 6

The course bundles 100 individually distinct AI-created reference images, one per lesson. The first 20 generated images are assigned one-to-one to specific lessons; 80 additional images were generated separately for their lesson topics. `unique-lesson-prompts.json` records the final prompts, assignments, source PNG locations, and SHA-256 for every bundled JPEG. `prompts.json` retains the original 20 prompts. No image is reused by a second lesson.

Source PNGs remain in the generated-images directory. JPEG copies preserve the original 4:3 composition at quality 90. The 100 offline images occupy about 47 MB. Every image was visually inspected; all files are also checked for distinct hashes and successful UIKit loading in the curriculum test.

The app clearly labels the collection as AI-created imaginary subjects. It encourages checking anatomy and physical details against life and personal photographs. These references support observation and composition practice and are not documentary or engineering sources. The original 20 lessons also retain their native construction diagrams as a separately labeled learning aid.

All 100 lessons include an original idea, observation prompt, preparation, developed demonstration, three exercise steps, common mistakes with fixes, an independent assignment, self-review, and an off-screen exercise. The new workshop sections alone contain 26,861 words, in addition to the existing lesson text. The curriculum uses observation, contour, negative space, proportion, value, form, perspective, and selective mark-making.

The following primary educational resources informed the organization:

- [National Gallery of Art — Explore the Basics of Drawing](https://www.nga.gov/educational-resources/explore-basics-drawing)
- [National Gallery of Art — Drawing Everyday Objects](https://www.nga.gov/educational-resources/lesson-drawing-everyday-objects)
- [Getty — Understanding Formal Analysis](https://www.getty.edu/education/teachers/building_lessons/formal_analysis.html)
- [Getty — Creating Form with Line](https://www.getty.edu/art/exhibitions/hatched/)

No images or lesson wording were downloaded from these sources, and the app does not claim their endorsement.
