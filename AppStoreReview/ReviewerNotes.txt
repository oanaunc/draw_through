DRAW THROUGH — NOTES FOR APP REVIEW
Version 1.0.0 (Build 1) — first submission

Draw Through is an offline drawing-reference tool for iPhone and iPad. It works
like a digital light table: the artist adds photos as reference layers, arranges
them into a composition, then rests paper on the screen and traces or draws from
what is shown underneath.

SIGN-IN
No account, sign-in, or demo credentials are required. Every feature is available
immediately on first launch.

NO NETWORK, NO PURCHASES
The app makes no network requests of any kind. There are no in-app purchases,
subscriptions, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Nothing is transmitted off
the device.

FIRST LAUNCH
A three-screen introduction appears. Tap "Continue" twice, then "Start Drawing"
to reach the main screen. The introduction can be replayed later from
Settings (gear icon) > About > Replay introduction.

HOW TO TEST THE CORE WORKFLOW
1. Tap "New Canvas", then "Create". The canvas opens.
2. Tap "Image" in the bottom bar (or tap the invitation in the middle of the
   empty canvas) and pick one or more photos using Apple's system photo picker.
3. Tap an image to select it. Drag to move, pinch to resize, rotate with two
   fingers, or drag a corner handle to resize.
4. "Layers" lists every image. From there you can rename, reorder, hide, lock,
   or delete a layer. Tap "Edit" to reorder or swipe to delete.
5. "Adjust" changes the selected image: Color / B&W / Outline, plus opacity,
   brightness, and contrast.
6. "View" toggles a faint grid over the canvas, changes the canvas background
   colour, and offers "Fit composition" and "Reset view".
7. "Lock" freezes the composition for tracing. To unlock, press and hold the
   round lock control near the bottom of the screen for two seconds — a ring
   fills as you hold. A single tap will not unlock it; this is deliberate, so a
   hand resting on the screen while drawing cannot disturb the composition.
8. The checkmark in the top bar saves manually. Compositions also save
   automatically after every change.
9. The share icon in the top bar opens Export, where the flattened composition
   can be shared or saved to Photos.
10. The back arrow returns to the project list. Projects can be favourited
    (heart), deleted (trash), or duplicated via long-press.

TWO THINGS THAT MAY LOOK LIKE BUGS
- "Adjust" is intentionally dimmed and unavailable until an image layer is
  selected, because it edits the selected layer. Tap an image first.
- Two-finger dragging on empty canvas space pans the infinite canvas. A
  one-finger drag on an image moves that image instead.

PERMISSIONS
Reference images are chosen through the system photo picker (PHPickerViewController),
which does not require photo library access. The only permission prompt appears
when the reviewer chooses "Save to Photos" in the Export panel, which requests
add-only photo library access.

DATA HANDLING
Projects, reference images, and settings are stored only in the app's own
container on the device and are deleted with the app. No user data is collected,
shared, or transmitted, which is why the App Privacy section is filed as
"Data Not Collected".

CONTENT
All artwork bundled with the app (app icon, introduction illustrations, and
sample reference photographs) is owned by the developer. Reviewer-selected
photos stay on the device and are never published by the app.
